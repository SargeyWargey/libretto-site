Requirements

  • iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later, including iPhone Duo.
  • Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later, paired with an iPhone that has Libretto.
  • Apple TV with tvOS 26 or later, signed in to the same Apple Account, with Sync with iCloud on.
  • Catch Me Up, Remind Me and Chapter Recaps need Apple Intelligence: an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M-series or A17 Pro chip, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Other devices show the recaps and Story Maps your devices with it have written, through iCloud.

Books Libretto can open

DRM-free EPUB, PDF, CBZ, FB2, TXT, Markdown, HTML and RTF, and M4B, M4A, MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF and CAF audio. Books protected with DRM by another store only open in that store’s app.

Libretto Pro

Libretto Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, and shared with your family through Family Sharing. There’s no subscription.
