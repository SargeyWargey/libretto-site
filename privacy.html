Privacy policy

Libretto collects no data about you. There’s no account, no analytics, no advertising and no Libretto server. This policy explains what stays on your device, what Sync with iCloud keeps in your own iCloud, and the few things that leave your device when you ask them to.

Last updated October 29, 2026. The same policy is in the app, in Settings → About.

What stays on your device

Your books and audiobooks, your place in each one, bookmarks, highlights and notes, your Notebook, your reading log and reading sessions, your goals and your settings are all stored on your iPhone or iPad. The only copy Libretto makes is the one Sync with iCloud keeps in your own iCloud account, described below. Your settings, such as page themes and fonts, stay on each device.

What Libretto works out on your device

  • Read-Along. Audiobooks are transcribed with Apple’s on-device speech recognition and matched to the ebook on your device. The audio and the transcript never leave it.
  • Catch Me Up and Remind Me. Recaps are written by Apple Intelligence’s on-device model from the pages you’ve already read. The book’s text isn’t sent anywhere.
  • Chapter Recaps and the Story Map. Each chapter’s recap, and the people and places in it, are worked out by the same on-device model as you finish the chapter, and kept beside the book on your device. They only leave it through Sync with iCloud, to your own iCloud, or if you send them to another device yourself, with Send to Another Device.
  • Read Aloud. Books are read with the voices installed on your device.

Sync with iCloud

Sync with iCloud keeps your library the same on every iPhone and iPad signed in to your Apple Account. It’s on from the start, and you can turn it off in Libretto’s Settings, under iCloud.

  • What it keeps in your iCloud: each book’s title, author and cover, your place and progress in it, your highlights, notes and bookmarks, the books you’ve finished, your reading log, sessions, goals and streak, Chapter Recaps and Story Maps, Read-Along pairs and their timings, the names and kinds of your devices, and which Hardcover books your books are matched to. It’s stored in your account’s private CloudKit database.
  • Books in iCloud: with Store Books in iCloud on, the files of books and audiobooks you add are kept in Libretto’s storage in your iCloud Drive, so your other devices can download them. It counts toward your iCloud storage, and you can turn it off in the same place.
  • Apple TV: once Libretto on an Apple TV signed in to your Apple Account has started, your iPhone also keeps each book’s text in your iCloud for it, and a smaller copy of any audiobook you ask to play on the TV, until you remove it.
  • Your Hardcover sign-in: if you connect Hardcover, its sign-in tokens are kept in iCloud Keychain, so your other devices are signed in too. Disconnecting removes them from every device.
  • Who can see it: it all lives in your own iCloud account, and only your devices signed in to that account can read it. It never reaches Libretto’s developer, who has no way to see it. Apple stores it under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Deleting it: turning Sync with iCloud off stops it on that device. To delete what’s in iCloud, open the Settings app → your name → iCloud, and choose Libretto from what’s using your storage.

Camera and face data

Blink to Turn Pages and Auto-Scroll’s eye features (pausing or listening when you look away, and Sleep Detection) use the front camera through Apple’s ARKit face tracking, and only while you’re using one of them in a book.

  • Each camera frame is examined on the device, as it arrives, for whether your eyes are open or closed and which way you’re facing.
  • No images, video or face data are recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and none of it is shared with anyone.
  • It’s used only to turn, scroll and pause pages, and to start or stop the narration. It’s never used for advertising, identification or any other purpose.

When something leaves your device

Only when you use a feature that needs another service:

  • Hardcover. If you connect a Hardcover account, Libretto reads your shelves, ratings and reading goals from it and sends your reading status, progress in pages and ratings to it. You sign in on Hardcover’s own page, and the sign-in tokens are kept in your device’s Keychain, and in iCloud Keychain with Sync with iCloud on. What Hardcover keeps is covered by Hardcover’s own privacy policy. You can disconnect in Settings → Hardcover at any time.
  • Free Classics. Searches and downloads go to Project Gutenberg’s public catalog.
  • Download from Link. Libretto fetches the file at the address you paste.
  • Book covers. Covers for books linked to Hardcover are downloaded from its image servers and kept on your device.

Apple features

  • Apple Watch. The watch app talks to Libretto on your iPhone directly. The books and audio it has are copied from your iPhone to your watch.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight. Libretto adds your books and highlights to Spotlight’s index on your device, so you can search for them. Requests you make through Siri are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Notifications. The Streak Reminder is scheduled on your device.
  • Purchases. If you buy Libretto Pro, the App Store handles the payment, and Libretto never sees your payment details.

Children

Libretto doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here and on Libretto’s website, with a new date at the top.
